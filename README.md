# Pip's Playroom

A toddler learning app for iPad/iPhone Safari. It covers colors, numbers, words, shapes and music in 13 activities. Everything is self-contained in `index.html`: the art is SVG, sounds are synthesized with Web Audio, and Pip's voice is the device's built-in text-to-speech.

## Put it on GitHub Pages
1. Upload these three files to the root of a repo (or a folder of an existing Pages repo):
   - `index.html` — the whole app
   - `sw.js` — optional, lets it work offline after the first visit
   - `apple-touch-icon.png` — optional, the home-screen icon
2. In the repo, go to Settings → Pages and deploy from your branch.
3. On the iPad, open the URL in Safari, then tap Share → **Add to Home Screen**. It then launches full-screen like an app.

## Grown-up settings
Press and hold the gear on the home screen for about 2 seconds. From there you can:
- add his name (Pip will cheer for him)
- pick a voice and set its speed and pitch
- set the volume and background music
- choose the challenge level, or let it adapt
- choose how the home button works inside games (tap twice is the default)
- set a play timer — when time is up, Pip plays a lullaby and says bye-bye; hold the moon button to keep playing
- hide activities
- reset stickers

**Better voice:** on the iPad, go to Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → English and download an *Enhanced* or *Premium* voice (Ava, Zoe or Samantha). Then choose it in Pip's settings.

**Lock him in:** go to Settings → Accessibility → Guided Access and turn it on. Then, inside the app, triple-click the top button.
